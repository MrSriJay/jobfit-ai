Read one image of a job post. Return the employer, the job title, and the job description.

Use only words visible in the image. Copy the posting itself: the role, responsibilities, requirements, and the short company introduction that belongs with it. Do not shorten that posting into a summary, and do not add requirements that are not written there.

Leave company empty when the employer is not visible. Leave title empty when the job title is not visible. Leave description empty when the image is not a job post or the description cannot be read.

Write the description as plain text in the language of the posting. Drop navigation, cookie notices, and apply buttons. Do not mention that the text came from an image.
